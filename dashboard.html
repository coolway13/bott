How to use your tracker

1. Follow the action. Scores and game status update every minute while the tracker runs. Use the date, team search, and filters to find a game.

2. Get a prediction. Upcoming regular-season games receive an automatic estimate from MLB team run totals through the previous day. At least 10 completed games are required. A saved prediction is preserved. If no prediction exists, you can still enter advanced inputs manually.

3. Check your results. Open My predictions after games finish. Automatic estimates use an untrained team run-strength model, without pitcher, lineup, injury, weather, or betting-odds adjustments. The original advanced-input model is available for manual predictions. Pregame probabilities do not change with live scores. Demo games never affect accuracy.

The tracker starts automatically when you log in. Keep your Mac awake and online for Discord updates. Each matchup has its own Discord card, updated in place with live stats. Discord reports observed changes; it does not accept chat commands.
